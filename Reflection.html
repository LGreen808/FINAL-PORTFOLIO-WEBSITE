Reflection

When I first heard about the web design program, I honestly did not want to join, it just did not feel like something for me. But my parents asked me to do it because they said that learning how to code would be good for me in the real world. I am glad that I took the program, my experience in the web design program has had its fair share of ups and downs but overall I had a good experience in the web design program and I am proud of how I have grown throught these past three years. When I first started this program I honestly thought that I was going to take it for one year then drop out, and I was defeatest about coding, I used to think "oh this is not for me I cannot really do this, its more for people who have a passion for this like Hayley" so I resigned myself and said it was very challenging for me, and it was but I should not have been defeatest. But now, even though this program is still challenging for me, I am not defeatest anymore, I put in more effort, I try to turn my work in on time more, and I care more. I would say two challenges for me were Sophomore Year and Junior Year. In Sophomore Year I fell into a slump in general and my work started to drop off, and in Junior Year I had horrible time management and was just completely lost. But I am proud to say that I have grown from that, even though it is not fixed, my time management is better, I am not in a slump anymore, and I use resoruces more, like AI and my friends and etc to help me out when I am confused. I am glad that I took the web design program, I am glad that I challenged myself, I am proud of how I have grown as a web designer and as a person throught this program, and I am even glad that I experienced all of the ups and downs because they made me into the person and web designer that I am today. Thank you Mr. Kilgore and Mrs. Lenoard you guys were great teachers :D
